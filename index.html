Team Planner

How to use Team Planner

Plan who works on which MVP, week by week, and spot early when someone is about to run out of work or is overbooked.

What’s on the page

  • Alerts: Act now lists who runs out of work next week, who is short of work this week and who is overbooked. Next 12 weeks shows what’s coming. MVPs lists late MVPs and BAC mismatches. Click an alert to open the person or MVP.
  • Releases & milestones: when each release starts, its code stops, and its system test periods. Purple lines mark code stops, and the shaded area between beta and final code stop is the bug-fix window. The blue line is today.
  • Load strips: booked days against available days for all testers, then all developers.
  • MVPs: the solid bar is dev weeks and the striped bar is test weeks, both taken from who is assigned. The dashed red line is the due date.
  • Developers and Testers: one row per team member, showing what they work on each week, their leave, and their flags.

Planning work

  • Pick a brush in the top bar (an MVP, Defects or Leave), then drag across a person’s weeks. Painting replaces what was there.
  • Hold Shift while dragging to add work alongside what’s already there, for example half on two MVPs.
  • Erase removes work and leave from the weeks you drag over.
  • With Select, click a week on a person’s row to set leave days (1–5) or change the % and week range of an assignment. Click a name or an MVP to edit it.
  • Leave painted with the brush counts as 5 days. Click the week to change it to fewer days.
  • Releases edits release dates and milestones. Settings holds the thresholds and import/export.

What the colours mean

  • Overbooked: more than 100% of the person’s capacity is booked that week.
  • Short of work: less than 50% booked (the threshold is set in Settings). Weeks fully on leave don’t count.
  • ⚑ The week before someone runs short of work, so you have a week to find them something.
  • Leave: the number shows the days off. Leave of 3 days or more is outlined.
  • Spare capacity: with “Show spare capacity” on, the free days (+Xd) show who could pick up defects.
  • Late: weeks of MVP work planned after its due date.

How things are worked out

  • Capacity is 5 days a week minus leave. Load adds up the % of every assignment and defect duty in the week.
  • Planned hours for an MVP are assigned % × available days × hours per day (8 by default). They’re compared with the BAC: amber if under it, red if more than 20% over.
  • An MVP’s due date is its target release’s beta code stop unless you set its own date. Any dev or test work after it makes the MVP late.

Your data

The plan is saved in this browser only. Nobody else sees your changes, and clearing site data deletes them. Use Settings → Export JSON to keep a copy or share it, and Import JSON to load one. Reset to sample plan brings back the example data, and Reset to empty starts over but keeps the releases.
